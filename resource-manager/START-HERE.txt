SCIENCE VAULT WA — VERSION 15 SMART RESOURCE MANAGER

1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click OPEN-RESOURCE-MANAGER.html.
3. Click Choose Resource Folder.
4. Select a resource folder.
5. The manager automatically guesses classifications.
6. Correct individual resources using dropdowns.
7. To classify many at once: tick/select them, choose Year/Course, Strand/Unit,
   Topic, Subtopic and/or Type under 'Apply to selected', then click Apply.
8. Export Catalogue CSV or resources.js.

No Python or PowerShell is required. Your files stay on your computer.
