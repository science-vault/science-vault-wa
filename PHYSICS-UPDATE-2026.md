## Content changes

- Each of the 44 modules receives one original extended-response practice problem with four worked marking points, a draft response box and a revealable solution. These are practice rubrics, not official SCSA marking keys.
- Year 11: graph fitting, uncertainty bars, systematic errors, heat-pump energy accounting, pipe pressure/displacement boundaries and element formation.
- Year 12: quantum wave evidence, polarisation, photon momentum/beam power, photoelectric graph analysis, energy-level transitions, ionisation, fluorescence, phosphorescence, X-rays, blackbody radiation, lasers, LEDs and photovoltaic cells.
- Electromagnetic applications: complete Faraday relation, motional geometry, hotplates, regenerative braking, cyclotron limits, synchrotrons and mass spectrometry.
- Six labelled SVG models accompany the existing screens.
- Clearly distinguish 2026 core content from older relativistic velocity-addition work and wider gravitational-energy extensions.
- Repair a JavaScript syntax error in the earlier projectile override. Subsequent live projectile expansions remain authoritative and retain their 23 screens.
- Add a Year 12 mastery retry control and unbiased Fisher–Yates question shuffling. Expand quantum and equilibrium banks for the new coverage.
- Scope mobile layout adjustments to Physics, retain existing wire callbacks and save written practice drafts locally.
