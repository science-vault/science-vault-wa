Report a problem, request a correction or contact the site about a resource.

Resource corrections

If a resource is incorrectly categorised, has a broken link, or needs updated attribution, please include the resource title and the page where you found it when contacting the site administrator.

Copyright and takedown requests

For copyright or removal requests, provide enough information to identify the material, explain your relationship to the work, and describe the requested action. Requests will be reviewed and material can be removed while an issue is investigated.

Contact method

A dedicated public contact address or contact form will be added here. Until then, copyright and contact information can be provided through the project administrator.
