# Human Biology lesson refresh — 26 October 2026

The Unit 3 and 4 player uses the approved purple and white website design: editorial headings, a page sidebar, full-width reading space, original figures, diagram enlargement, a complete-page reading view and a progress footer.

The source layout still contains 79 lessons and 1,075 pages. Slide text regions and tables are converted to native website content. Standalone illustrations remain with their original page. No lesson IDs, progress indices or assessment bank data changed.

## Content review

32 explanation topics are added at 88 relevant page locations. These are part of the lesson body, and are also collected in each relevant lesson's SCSA study guide. The added material explains causal links, comparisons, evidence interpretation and limitations, rather than simply repeating syllabus dot points.

Direct text corrections address thyroid hormone classification, the misleading tetanus natural-immunity example and embryonic stem-cell potency. Text tables become native tables or typed content, with corrections applied to their teaching explanations. Repeated Learning Aim footer instructions are removed from the reading text to give space to teaching content. Full original source files are not modified.
