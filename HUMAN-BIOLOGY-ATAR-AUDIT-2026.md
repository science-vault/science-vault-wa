# Human Biology ATAR course audit — 8 October 2026

This is an independent content and functionality audit against the current SCSA course. It is not SCSA certification or endorsement.

## Result

| Course | Modules | Screens | Original written application tasks |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Year 11 | 41 | 972 | 234 |
| Year 12 | 60 | 1,717 | 208 |
| Total | 101 | 2,689 | 442 |

Year 11 includes 19 Unit 1 modules, 19 Unit 2 modules and 3 inquiry modules. Year 12 includes 35 preserved Unit 3 modules, 3 repaired inquiry modules and 22 new Unit 4 modules. Both exceed the corresponding Physics module count (24 and 20). Every module contains more than 20 screens. Screens are teaching, model analysis, formative checks, question attempts, worked solutions or source/evidence guidance; visits do not establish mastery.

Existing functioning Year 12 lecture screens and callable interactive wiring are retained. The baseline audit found three broken scripts and no working Unit 4 or Year 11 course selector. Those failures and gaps are addressed in this update.

## Corrections and qualifications

- Repair malformed HTML-string endings in the inquiry script, an unescaped apostrophe in deep endocrine content and invalid escaping in the endocrine-image patch. Cache versions are updated.
- Correct a nerve-impulse image link using `.png` when the repository asset is `.jpg`; validate all 620 unique image paths against the complete Human Biology asset tree.
- Replace the all-amines-water-soluble generalisation with explicit thyroid T3/T4 exceptions.
- Correct calcitonin explanations: inhibition of bone resorption and its limited normal adult role; identify PTH/active vitamin D as major adult regulators.
- Specify expression-compatible, commonly intron-free coding DNA for bacterial recombinant hormone production.
- Qualify insulin uptake as tissue-specific and distinguish hepatic blood-glucose buffering from muscle-local glycogen use.
- Distinguish rapid potassium-channel-mediated repolarisation from pump maintenance of gradients; stronger stimulation can change spike frequency rather than amplitude.
- Correct source-slide misconceptions about Sertoli-cell origins, histone modification, intestinal lipid products and universal cycle timing.
- Distinguish red-cell and plasma compatibility, and explain Rh sensitisation rather than assuming innate anti-D.
- Explain normally separate placental circulation without an absolute claim that no cellular transfer ever occurs; fetal shunt closure timing is variable.
- Distinguish stem-cell/gene-therapy potential from established universal cures and screening from definitive diagnosis.
- Use branching, mosaic human evolution, approximate dated ranges and context-dependent sickle-cell effects; avoid an obligatory brain-size progress ladder or exclusive early-tool-maker claim.
- Carbon-14 is not a universal multi-million-year fossil method; volcanic-layer dates can provide contextual brackets.

## Student assessment behaviour

Worked questions require an attempt before the next-screen solution. Independent questions have an answer box and explicit reveal control. Suggested marking points include allocations and browser-saved self-credit. These are self-assessments, not automatically graded or teacher-verified written results. Concept checks randomise options and require all answers correct; unanswered attempts cannot pass. Typed drafts and navigation are saved in the same browser. Existing lesson tracking start/stop integration is preserved.

The SCSA Year 12 external examination structure should be checked through the current course page: multiple choice 30%, short answer 50%, extended answer 20%, with 10 minutes reading and three hours working. The lesson tasks are topic practice, not claims to reproduce a complete official examination.
