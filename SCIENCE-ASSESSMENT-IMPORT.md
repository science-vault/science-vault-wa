## Coverage and remaining work

`science-assessment-import-status.json` is the source inventory and audit trail. It contains 2,009 files, including keys and supporting material: 1,671 distinct blobs and 338 exact duplicate files. These are file counts, not question counts. Twenty-eight distinct source/key blobs are referenced by the five batches. Fifteen student-paper blobs have been imported; 1,656 other distinct blobs remain to be triaged, extracted, or checked for question variants. Older key documents may include extra or changed questions, so key-only files are marked key_reference_pending_variant_review rather than counted as fully imported. Duplicate aliases are identified without re-importing identical source files. Existing lesson checks and authored equivalents are separate bank content and do not count as original extractions.

After batch 5, the shared lower Science bank has 4,140 questions: Year 7 — 1,089; Year 8 — 1,007; Year 9 — 994; Year 10 — 1,050. Of 1,843 MC records, 1,827 are battle eligible. Only 301 of the overall bank records belong to these original extraction batches.
