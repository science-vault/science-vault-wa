## Marking keys

Use supplied keys when suitable. Create missing keys and identify them as **Learning Vault created**, including mark allocations and acceptable alternatives. Preserve supplied keys separately when adding corrections. There are 17 new keys for the Year 7 Forces paper and 13 supplementary guides across the three batches.
