The course has been reviewed against the current SCSA ATAR Year 11 syllabus effective from 2026. These 167 editorial checks identify concrete teaching, tables and questions. They are not an official SCSA checklist, endorsement or proof of student mastery.

Actual practical competence requires teacher-approved investigations and observed student work. The investigation workshop supplies a method, risk considerations, synthetic teaching data, calculations and reporting tasks; reading it does not establish that competence.
