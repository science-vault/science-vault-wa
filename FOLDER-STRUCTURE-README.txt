RESOURCE FOLDER RULES

Years 7–10:
resources > Year > Science Strand > Resource Type

ATAR:
resources > ATAR > Subject > Year > Unit > Resource Type

Every resource location is split into:
- PowerPoints
- Worksheets
- Notes
- Practicals
- Revision
- Tests
- Other Resources

Exams are stored separately:
- Years 7–10: exams > Year
- ATAR: exams > ATAR > Subject > Year > Semester

Do not manually create Topic/Subtopic folders.
Topic and Subtopic will be handled later by the resource catalogue/database.

.keep files preserve empty folders in GitHub.
