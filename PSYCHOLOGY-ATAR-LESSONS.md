# Psychology ATAR focused lessons — audit 8 October 2026

The original 19 broad modules have been rebuilt as 34 Year 11 and 39 Year 12 focused lessons. Smaller lessons isolate mechanisms, studies and research skills so students can revisit a specific difficulty. Lesson length follows the topic rather than an artificial screen quota: 11–22 meaningful screens per lesson. The complete pair of courses contains 1,133 screens, 45,557 words of explicit theory (excluding captions, reference tables and written solutions), 279 written tasks, 97 explained SVG models and three retained interactive models.

## Retention and progress

Every original teaching page, comparison reference and interactive screen is relocated exactly once. All original 95 concept questions remain as broader topic reviews; 73 new focused distinctions provide formative checkpoints and shuffled concept practice. The 168 concept items are revision extras, not a simulation of the written ATAR examination. Written answers are self-assessed, not automatically graded.

Original question drafts and self-credit are migrated from their broad parent lesson when a focused lesson is first opened. Earlier visited-screen indices and mastery flags are not imported, since they would falsely imply that new teaching was completed. New drafts, progress and self-credit remain saved in that browser. These records are not teacher-verified achievement or cross-device account storage.

## Accuracy emphasis

- CNS/PNS versus functional divisions; reflex initiation versus later conscious awareness; myelin versus neurotransmitter.
- Contralateral body control versus visual-field routing; distributed language functions; imaging measures and resolution trade-offs.
- Developmental variability, five overlapping plasticity processes, schema revision and task-specific Piaget evidence.
- Bowlby’s non-deterministic working models; specified Harlow 1958 comparison; reunion-based A/B/C interpretation; within-culture variation and deprivation confounds.
- Attitude components versus functions; exact forced-compliance justification; situational attribution, self/group-serving biases; contact conditions and superordinate goals.
- Correct Milgram/Asch denominators; specified smoke-filled-room study rather than the separate intercom study; helping actions versus altruistic motives.
- Approximate/task-dependent memory-store estimates; 1974 working-memory model versus 2000 buffer; implicit/explicit dissociations and Molaison’s anatomical limits.
- Craik–Tulving experiment two; clear proactive/retroactive direction; recognition and relearning denominators; distinct causes of memory loss.
- Reinforcement/punishment by observed effect, positive/negative by addition/removal; interval availability still needs an eligible response.
- Bandura 1961 live-model procedure distinct from later filmed/consequence studies; learning versus immediate performance.
- Identified/integrated motivation remains extrinsic; autonomy is volition; Maslow versions and overlapping needs.
- Diener versus Ryff; subjective does not mean automatically invalid; before/after scores alone do not establish causation.
- GAS without inevitable exhaustion, weighted life changes without diagnosis, appraisal/coping/reappraisal with contextual fit.
- Current three-stage NREM classification; variable cycle architecture; phone-restriction package distinct from isolated blue light.

The He trial was checked against its primary report (38 participants; 30-minute pre-bed restriction; four weeks): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7010281/. Current CTE confirmation limits checked against NIH, and thiamine-deficiency explanation against NIAAA. Educational cases do not diagnose individuals or require students to disclose personal health information. Historical harmful procedures are analysed rather than reproduced.

Seven added models explain groupthink feedback, attachment variation, plasticity processes, conformity denominators, privacy distinctions, memory-loss pathways and nonlinear relationships. All models include reading guidance and limitations; numerical examples clearly labelled fictional. Existing 90 models retained.
