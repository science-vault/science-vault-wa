Human Biology ATAR Year 12: coverage map

Authored Year 12 course, expanded against the SCSA 2026-applicable and published 2027 syllabus content. This maps teaching content; practical investigation delivery and teacher assessment remain part of the school program.

60 authored lessons: 35 Unit 3, 22 Unit 4 and three inquiry lessons. Lesson text includes explanations, examples, practice and diagrams. Image references appear at the end of each lesson.
