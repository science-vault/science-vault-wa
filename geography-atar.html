Learn, practise and review

Each focused lesson begins with a Do Now and intentions, then I do explanations, a map or diagram, formative checks and an attempted worked model with a marking table. We do evidence interpretation and interactive practice prepare you for You do written tasks, retrieval and a mastery concept check.

Use geographical language, units, mapped evidence and justified conclusions. Typed answers save on this browser. Print practice includes lined writing space and a map/diagram; Print marking guide gives a separate step-by-step table. Concept mastery and self-assessed marks are practice evidence, not a teacher-verified exam grade.

Year 11 depth sequences cover Yasi and dengue, coffee and football. Year 12 covers climate change plus a separately labelled biodiversity option, and Perth/Mumbai transport and water challenges. Follow your teacher’s chosen depth study. Core mapping, statistics, remote sensing, GIS and ethical fieldwork are taught and revisited.

Original classroom values are labelled; official sources supply real case context. Schematic locator maps are teaching aids, with their limits stated. Use official maps, image products and local fieldwork for full inquiries.
