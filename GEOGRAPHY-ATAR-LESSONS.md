# Geography ATAR — 2026 lesson coverage

56 Year 11 lessons; 73 Year 12 lessons; 532 written tasks including worked models.

Each lesson: Do Now; explicit concept and method; relevant diagram; source-aware case; formative checks; attempted I do model and step-by-step marking table; guided We do interpretation and interactive practice; three or more You do tasks; printable writing space and separate key; retrieval, transfer and a four-question mastery concept check.

Units 1 and 2 use Yasi/dengue and coffee/football as teaching depth-study sequences. Unit 3 provides climate change with a separately labelled optional biodiversity sequence. Unit 4 covers Perth and Mumbai with two challenges each (transport and water), two strategies for each challenge and sustainability/liveability evaluation. Real context is linked to dated official sources; numerical scenarios are labelled classroom assumptions. Country comparisons and local demographic inquiries include source-led research tasks, not invented national statistics. Teachers should select and contextualise inquiry/fieldwork evidence.

Core mapping, remote sensing, statistics, GIS and ethical fieldwork skills are taught in Year 11 and revisited in Year 12. Reference Geography 2026 syllabus mapping/inquiry lists when scheduling repeated practice in every unit; skills are also embedded in content lessons. Unit 4 uses the descriptive navigation title “Planning sustainable places” for SCSA’s Places and their challenges.

## Validation and boundaries

Original suggested marking points accept equivalent justified answers. Marks are self-assessed; concept mastery and screen visits do not establish written exam mastery. Typed drafts save locally. Existing class completion remains student-reported through the site’s class workflow. Printable map sources remain visible; blank sketch boxes accompany relevant tasks.
