## Corrections and clarifications

- Currency conversion specifies invoice currency, AUD quote direction, conversion timing and the effects of appreciation/depreciation.
- Forward foreign exchange contracts are distinguished from customer sales contracts; options give a right rather than an obligation and have a cost.
- Documentary collections do not ordinarily guarantee payment. Documentary letters of credit depend on complying documents and bank terms.
- Retained earnings are distinguished from available cash. Credit unions may be authorised deposit-taking institutions.
- Financial examples use a consistent dataset and all six syllabus formulas. Return on equity uses closing equity. Percentage ratios explicitly multiply by 100. No universal industry margin benchmark is presented as a rule.
- ILO conventions, recommendations and domestic implementation are distinguished. Outdated child labour counts are removed; safety and health is included among fundamental principles.
- Culture and leadership material avoids categorical claims about people based on nationality. All seven specified leadership traits are taught.
- Kotter step 8 is anchoring approaches in culture, correcting the incomplete source label. Lewin Forcefield Analysis is distinguished from the three-stage change model.
- HTTPS is explained as a connection-security control, not proof of an honest website or secure stored data.
- FTA preferences require eligibility and origin checks; historic trade figures and blanket claims that all goods become cheaper are removed.
