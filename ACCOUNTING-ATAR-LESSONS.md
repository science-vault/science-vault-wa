# Accounting and Finance ATAR — 2026 lesson coverage

115 focused lessons across Years 11 and 12. Original examples and suggested exam marking guides; not official SCSA keys.

Each lesson: retrieval Do Now → intentions and success criteria → explicit input → annotated model → formative check → teacher worked example → guided reconstruction → independent written practice → closure/extension → mastery check. Typed drafts and self-assessed marking points save locally; students can print lined practice with a separate marking table.

GST applies in Unit 1 examples only where stated. Unit 2 adjustment tasks and Year 12 tasks exclude GST. Year 11 and Year 12 return-on-assets/profit ratio conventions differ; the lessons state the applicable definition. All company tax rates are supplied fictional task assumptions.
