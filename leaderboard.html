How XP and rankings work

Attempt a different question: 4 XP. An automatically checked correct answer earns up to 20 XP in total. Complete a different lesson: up to 70 XP. Repeating the same result gives no extra XP. Improving an answer earns only the difference.

Subject rankings include only XP earned in that subject. Students with equal XP share a rank. Rankings are visible within your enrolled classes and are participation records, separate from school marks and Learning Quest story bonuses.
