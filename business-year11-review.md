# Year 11 Business Management and Enterprise lesson review

Reviewed 26 October 2026.

35 supplied topic collections have been converted to native website lessons. Six additional lessons address the identified curriculum gaps. The resulting 41 lessons contain 548 reading pages in a teaching sequence across Unit 1 and Unit 2. Year 12 lessons are retained.

The student interface contains no document names, staff names, classroom housekeeping or raster captures of teaching pages. Text and tables are website content. Image-based source information was inspected using OCR; noisy results were rewritten as checked explanations rather than published verbatim.

## Curriculum and factual review

The baseline is the SCSA ATAR Year 11 syllabus for teaching from 2026. Added coverage addresses economic factors, the complete marketing mix, research and case analysis, responsible marketing, WA work health and safety and profitability. Employment standards, intellectual property, consumer law and contract explanations were updated to avoid carrying forward dated or overly broad claims. Numerical financial examples are illustrative.

Sources: [SCSA](https://senior-secondary.scsa.wa.edu.au/syllabus-and-support-materials/humanities-and-social-sciences/business-management-and-enterprise), [Fair Work Ombudsman](https://www.fairwork.gov.au/employment-conditions/national-employment-standards), [Fair Work Commission](https://www.fwc.gov.au/what-minimum-period-employment), [ACCC](https://www.accc.gov.au/consumers/buying-products-and-services/consumer-rights-and-guarantees), [WorkSafe WA](https://www.worksafe.wa.gov.au/workers), [IP Australia](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/understanding-ip/types-of-ip), [Attorney-General copyright guidance](https://www.ag.gov.au/rights-and-protections/copyright/copyright-owners).
