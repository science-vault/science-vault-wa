How to use these lessons

Mathematics Specialist builds on Mathematics Methods. Revise the relevant algebra, differentiation, integration and probability from Methods as needed. Keep written working: a numerical answer alone often does not communicate the required reasoning.

Draft answers and progress are saved on the current browser. Suggested marking points support self-review; written answers need comparison with the supplied solution rather than an automatic mark.

Aligned to the SCSA Year 11 syllabus for teaching from 2026 and Year 12 syllabus for teaching from 2025. The published 2027 Year 12 numbered content was compared during the audit. Questions are original ATAR-style practice.
