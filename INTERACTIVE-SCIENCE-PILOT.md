# Mixture Mission — Year 7 interactive pilot

Open `interactive-science-pilot.html`, linked from the home page and Year 7 Science page.

Original approximately 45-minute SCSA 2026 Year 7 Chemical Sciences lesson: launch, I Do, We Do, You Do and exit ticket. Includes an accessible conceptual filtration model, four multiple-choice checks, a three-step method challenge, written observation/explanation/reflection, self-check, teaching guide and suggested rubric.

## Pilot boundaries

No account is required. The pilot records responses only in this browser under `sv-mixture-mission-pilot-v1`. It does not send responses to Firebase or create a teacher dashboard, and it does not award platform XP. Students export a plain-text record or print it for teacher review. Written responses are recorded, not automatically graded. The teaching guide is public lesson guidance, not a secured marking-key area.

Original diagrams are conceptual and not to scale. Optional practical work requires teacher supervision and the school's risk assessment. Ordinary filtration is not a guarantee of potable water.

## Validation

`node --test tests/interactive-science-pilot.test.cjs`

Covers restoration validation, full completion, score boundaries, ungraded written responses and first/corrected attempt reporting. Browser checks cover retry feedback, all three model samples, guided method, completion, response restoration, edited-answer invalidation, teaching guide and export.
