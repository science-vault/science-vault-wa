# Business Management and Enterprise ATAR — 2026 lesson sequence

102 focused lessons. Original teaching text, hypothetical business cases and teacher-created suggested marking guides; not official SCSA marking keys.

I–We–You: retrieval, intentions, explicit input, causal model, formative check, worked example, interactive guided decision pathway, independent case evaluation, misconception correction, extended report practice, closure and mastery. Writing space scales from 10 to 30 lines. Marking guides award separate points for concepts, application, analysis and justified judgement. Accept equivalent supported conclusions.

Year 12 ratio conventions follow BME: return on equity uses equity at end, not average equity. Ratios shown as percentages where appropriate. Exchange rates explicitly state foreign currency per AUD. Figures are supplied classroom assumptions. No actual minimum wage or current tariff is invented. Copyright is automatic; it is not registered through IP Australia. Australian Standards are not all mandatory; mandatory safety standards must be followed. Minimum wages and unfair dismissal are not simply additional NES items. WTO trade remedies are distinguished from national foreign-policy sanctions. Legal teaching summaries should be checked against the linked regulator and applicable jurisdiction.
