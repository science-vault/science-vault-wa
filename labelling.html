About the activities and curriculum grouping

The labelling questions are created for Learning Vault. Detailed anatomy, structure and equipment diagrams are sourced online; image credits are listed below. Graphs and concept models are authored for practice. The interaction is inspired by diagram-labelling practice such as ThatQuiz; its diagrams and questions have not been copied.

Lower-school Science placements use the WA 2026 topic sequence. Other subject/year tags are practice placements, rather than a claim that every syllabus dot point is assessed. Teachers should choose activities that match their program.
