Question 1[13 marks]
Physics ATAR • Problem Solving • Vertical motion
A girl throws a table tennis ball vertically upwards at 5.00 m s⁻¹ towards the ceiling, 1.00 m above the point of release, and catches the ball 50.0 cm below the point of release. The collision with the ceiling is elastic. Ignore air resistance.
(a) Determine the speed at which the table tennis ball hits the ceiling.
(3 marks)
speed __________________ m s⁻¹
(b) Calculate the time of flight from release to catch.
(5 marks)
time __________________ s
(c) Draw a velocity–time graph from release to catch. Indicate a suitable scale on both axes.
(5 marks)

(1 mark)
(b) Explain how the magnetic force direction can be determined.

Question 3[6 marks]
Physics ATAR • Comprehension • Special relativity
A spacecraft passes Earth at relativistic speed. Use the information in the diagram and your knowledge of reference frames to answer the questions.

Marking Key

Question 1 — Problem Solving

(a) Award marks for correct constant-acceleration method, substitution and speed.
(b) Treat the motion in sections and sum the relevant time intervals.
(c) Correct velocity–time graph has gradient −g on each free-flight section and an instantaneous reversal of velocity at the elastic ceiling collision.

Question 2 — Short Response

Use the magnetic-field direction and the required force direction with the motor effect/Fleming’s left-hand rule to determine conventional current. Award explanation marks for correctly linking field, current and force.

Question 3 — Comprehension

Credit responses that correctly identify the relevant inertial frames, distinguish proper time from dilated time, and apply special-relativity reasoning consistently.
